🍾 Bottle shops in the Quarter

Where to buy a cold can to walk with. City ordinance 54-404 bans opened glass on any street, sidewalk or park between Canal, Rampart, Esplanade and the river, which is the whole Quarter. Cans and plastic cups are fine. A second rule, 54-406, stops a shop letting you leave with a can it has already opened for you, so buy it sealed and open it once you are outside. That is the whole trick.

Outside the French Quarter

Four areas, each worth a half-day, sorted by how far you have to go. Frenchmen St you can walk to; the Garden District, City Park and the CBD want a streetcar or a ~10-minute rideshare. Each block below carries its own eating, drinking and doing, so you can plan one trip at a time instead of criss-crossing the city.
