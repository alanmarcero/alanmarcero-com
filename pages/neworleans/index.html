One list for everywhere you can eat, drink or buy a cold can — the Quarter’s dining rooms, its coffee counters, its bakeries, its breakfast rooms, its bottle shops, the barbecue that is mostly not in the Quarter at all, and the old bars — 114 places, nearest to the hotel first. They were eight separate lists once, so you could look at courtyards or look at breakfast but never ask for both at once.

Every pill narrows what is left. Press ☕ Coffee, then 🌳 Courtyard, then 🍳 Breakfast and you are asking for a place that is all three, not any of the three. The number on a pill is what you would be left with if you pressed it, so a pill that would empty the list greys out first, and a pill you have already pressed shows a ✕ to take it back off. Price is the exception — $ and $$ together mean either, and only the 52 rooms carrying a Yelp price level answer those pills at all.

All 4★ or better on Yelp except where a card says otherwise: Felix’s (3.9) and Royal House (3.8) were asked for by name, and the coffee counters keep their real ratings.

📌 Notes on the list

Where to buy a cold can to walk with. City ordinance 54-404 bans opened glass on any street, sidewalk or park between Canal, Rampart, Esplanade and the river, which is the whole Quarter. Cans and plastic cups are fine. A second rule, 54-406, stops a shop letting you leave with a can it has already opened for you, so buy it sealed and open it once you are outside. That is the whole trick.
