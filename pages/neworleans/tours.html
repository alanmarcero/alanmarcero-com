The Magazine St Food Tours

← Back to Main Family Itinerary

The gator tour is booked. Everything here walks Magazine St or the Garden District — that rule now covers every section, food tours and budget walks alike, and the French Quarter is off the page. Priced for 3 adults and a 7-year-old: two food tours at $376 and $426, and three ways to see the same streets for $60 to $250.

🍽️ 1. Food tours

Priced for 3 adults and a 7-year-old, cheapest first. Both walk Magazine St, which is the whole point: the two operators that only run the French Quarter were dropped. Doctor Gumbo has six tours and every one of them is in the Quarter; Destination Kitchen does go to the Garden District, but only on a 21+ cocktail-and-architecture walk, not a food tour. Stops below are each operator's own published list — one names them, one refuses, and the card says which.

What the $39 seat actually is
🧒 “Reduced – Non Eater” · phone only
Their booking system does not call it a child ticket. It is a non-eater fare, and it exists on every departure — it is simply never shown to an online buyer.
◆ Their own words: “Children 12 and under who share portions with a parent qualify for a discounted ticket at $39, available by calling our office directly. If your child is a big eater, a full-priced ticket may be the better call.”
◆ So it is a sharing fare, not a discount. He eats off your plates for $39, or eats his own for $129. Worth deciding before you dial, because the $90 gap is the whole saving on this page.
◆ The other seats: $129 is the non-refundable ticket — no refund, no change. $149 buys a full refund up to 24 hours out. That is $20 a head for a cancellable trip.
The traps
🚩 Three things that look like deals
Two of these are the top Google results for this tour, and both are wrong in a way you only discover at checkout.
◆ The “$75” on NOLA Adventures is dead. Their page still headlines $75 and a $450 private tour. The booking widget on that page is New Orleans Secrets’ own FareHarbor calendar — item 51615, the same inventory as the operator’s site — and it prices at $129 / $149 / $629. The headline was never updated; the checkout is the truth.
◆ They are on Groupon — with a different tour. The listing is the Small-Group French Quarter History Walking Tour from $49, a two-hour history walk. The Magazine St foodie tour is not sold there, so Groupon’s site-wide codes cannot reach it.
◆ Not on Go City. The New Orleans pass covers the School of Cooking demo and the Southern Food & Beverage Museum, not this tour. No pass, city card or bundle includes it.

💸 3. Budget tours

The Underground Donut Tour you did last time now prices at $250 for 3 adults and a 7-year-old — $65 an adult, $55 for a child 10 or under, read off its own checkout on 4 Sept 2026. Everything here comes in at or under that, cheapest first, and the Magazine and Garden District rule applies here too: four cheaper walks came off this list for being French Quarter — two Viator food walks at about $120, Free Tours by Foot’s Quarter food tour, and the School of Cooking’s $40 demo on St Louis St. Only the donut tour involves eating; in this neighbourhood, at this money, the rest are walks.

Cheapest
Free Tours by Foot — Garden District
📍 Prytania St & Washington Ave · Sun, Mon, Thu, Sat
Pay-what-you-wish: the walk costs nothing to book and you hand the guide whatever it was worth at the end. Two hours, about a mile, entirely inside the district.
What you walk past
Colonel Short’s Villa and its cornstalk fence, the Briggs-Staub House, Toby’s Corner — the oldest house in the district — and Commander’s Palace. Lafayette Cemetery No. 1 is shut for maintenance, so it is told from the gates rather than walked.

💡 4. Before you book
  • 🧒 Both have a child rate — that is new. Sidewalk charges $79 for ages 4–17 and nothing under 4; New Orleans Secrets does $39 for under-12s sharing portions, by phone on 504-517-5397, not online. The two French Quarter tours that came off this page charged him a full adult fare, so the shortlist got cheaper per head as it got shorter.
  • 💵 $50 apart, and the gap is the group size. $376 against $426. Sidewalk is a standard walk with five stops; Secrets caps at nine guests, covers gratuities and taxes, pours a beer, and serves full portions rather than tastes.
  • 📉 Read the review counts, not just the stars. 4.7 on 26 reviews is a thinner promise than 4.9 on 304. Sidewalk answers that by naming its venues — its six carry about 3,500 Yelp reviews between them, which is the number to weigh.
  • 🏷️ Groupon reaches neither of them. New Orleans Secrets is on Groupon, but only with its $49 French Quarter history walk — the Magazine food tour is not sold there. Sidewalk’s $89 was compared against a $79 direct price that turns out to be the child fare, and the adult fare is $99, so that one is worth re-pricing before you book. Full workings in the discount hunt.
  • 🗺️ One list is a plan, not a promise. Sidewalk visits five of the six it names and says the line-up can change; Secrets will not say where it goes at all. Neither guarantees a given stop on a given day.
