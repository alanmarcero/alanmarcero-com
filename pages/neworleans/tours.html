🍽️ 1. Food tours

Priced for 3 adults and a 7-year-old, cheapest first. Where an operator runs several, this page takes the Magazine St tour, then the Garden District, then the French Quarter — and no cocktail tours. Each card says which tour it is and where it walks. Stops are the operator's own published list; two of the four decline to name theirs, and the card says so rather than guessing.

🍛
Destination Kitchen — French Quarter
📍 French Quarter · meeting point sent with the voucher
⭐ 4.8 (201)
Their only food tour that is not a cocktail crawl. Small groups at 9:30, 11:30 and 2:00; TripAdvisor ranked it #1 food tour in New Orleans.
Five to seven tastings · venues not named
The operator publishes the menu, not the addresses. Gumbo or jambalaya, alligator bites, the muffuletta USA Today wrote up, hand-pulled pralines, a char-grilled oyster and a Creole confection — plus tastings in the French Market. The meeting point comes with the voucher, and they warn stops may swap without notice.

Doctor Gumbo — French Quarter
📍 French Quarter · meets at 3rd Block Depot, 316 Chartres St
⭐ 4.9 (156)
The Food History Tour — their cocktail and combo tours are skipped here. USA Today put it in the country's top ten. Vegetarian option on request; no gluten-free or vegan.
Up to five stops · one named, four by type
3rd Block Depot⭐ 3.7 · 90
316 Chartres St · Where the tour meets. Yelp orders the beignets and the bananas foster French toast; the room itself is a brunch-and-cocktails counter.
The other four are described, not named: two Creole restaurants, a sandwich shop, a praline and candy store, and a hot sauce bar. What lands on the table is gumbo, boudin, po'boys, muffuletta and pralines.

Sidewalk — Lower Garden District
📍 Lower Garden District · the foot of Magazine St
⭐ 4.7 (26)
They also run a French Quarter walk; this is the Magazine St one, so it is the one taken here. Fridays and Saturdays, 11–2. The only tour on this page that names where it goes.
Five of these six · best-rated first
Turkey and the Wolf⭐ 4.4 · 1,121
739 Jackson Ave · Bon Appétit's 2017 Best New Restaurant in America, and the highest-reviewed room on this list. Sandwiches played for laughs and taken seriously.
Stein's Market and Deli⭐ 4.4 · 823
2207 Magazine St · Jewish-Italian deli. Yelp's dish list runs Rachel, Reuben, Fernando, roast beef — in that order.
Piety and Desire Chocolate⭐ 4.8 · 48
2032 Magazine St · The city's only bean-to-bar chocolate maker. Best-rated stop here, on the fewest reviews.
Juan's Flying Burrito⭐ 4.0 · 927
2018 Magazine St · Self-described Creole taqueria. The Flying Burrito and the blackened fish tacos are what Yelp photographs.
Coco Korea BBQ⭐ 4.0 · 496
2240 Magazine St · Korean barbecue with grills and vent hoods in the tables, ordered volcano-style to share.
Elizabeth Street Cafe⭐ 4.0 · 73
1507 Magazine St · French-Vietnamese cafe and bakery beside the Hotel Saint Vincent — banh mi, spring rolls, a patio.

New Orleans Secrets — Magazine St
📍 Magazine St · meets outside Mahony's, 3454 Magazine St
⭐ 4.9 (304)
The Magazine Street Foodie Tour, picked over their French Quarter and Uptown walks. Five stops, seven dishes minimum, nine guests maximum, one beer or soda included. Half a mile. Not suitable under 6; he is 7.
Five stops · deliberately unpublished
They will not say where they go. In their own words: “because we love anticipation (and because the tour may change on a given day), we do not release where we go or what we eat.” The menu runs to beef, pork, seafood and vegetables, in full portions rather than tastes. 💡 2. Before you book
  • 🏷️ Groupon still loses on three of the four. Destination Kitchen is $82–89 there against $76–83 direct, Doctor Gumbo is $85 either way, and New Orleans Secrets has no listing at all. Sidewalk is the open question: Groupon’s $89 was compared against a $79 direct price that turns out to be the child fare, and the adult fare is $99 — so that one is worth re-pricing before you book.
  • 🧒 Two of the four have a child rate, not one. Sidewalk charges $79 for ages 4–17 and nothing under 4; New Orleans Secrets does $39 for under-12s sharing portions, by phone on 504-517-5397, not online. On Destination Kitchen and Doctor Gumbo he pays a full adult fare.
  • 📉 Read the review counts, not just the stars. Tastebud (87) came off this page for being thin, and Sidewalk stays by request on 26. Where an operator names its stops you can check the rooms instead of the tour: Sidewalk’s six carry about 3,500 Yelp reviews between them.
  • 🗺️ Two operators will not tell you where they go. Destination Kitchen publishes a menu and sends the meeting point with the voucher; New Orleans Secrets says outright that it keeps the stops back. Both also reserve the right to swap a stop on the day, so treat every list here as the plan, not the promise.
