The Magazine St Food Tours

The gator tour is booked. Two food tours left, both on Magazine St, priced for 3 adults and a 7-year-old. An operator only makes this page if it runs a food tour outside the French Quarter — which took Destination Kitchen and Doctor Gumbo off it.

🍽️ 1. Food tours

Priced for 3 adults and a 7-year-old, cheapest first. Both walk Magazine St, which is the whole point: the two operators that only run the French Quarter were dropped. Doctor Gumbo has six tours and every one of them is in the Quarter; Destination Kitchen does go to the Garden District, but only on a 21+ cocktail-and-architecture walk, not a food tour. Stops below are each operator's own published list — one names them, one refuses, and the card says which.

📍 Lower Garden District · the foot of Magazine St
⭐ 4.7 (26)
They also run a French Quarter walk; this is the Magazine St one, so it is the one taken here. Fridays and Saturdays, 11–2. The only tour on this page that names where it goes.

📍 Magazine St · meets outside Mahony's, 3454 Magazine St
⭐ 4.9 (304)
The Magazine Street Foodie Tour, picked over their French Quarter and Uptown walks. Five stops, seven dishes minimum, nine guests maximum, one beer or soda included. Half a mile. Not suitable under 6; he is 7.

💡 2. Before you book
  • 🧒 Both have a child rate — that is new. Sidewalk charges $79 for ages 4–17 and nothing under 4; New Orleans Secrets does $39 for under-12s sharing portions, by phone on 504-517-5397, not online. The two French Quarter tours that came off this page charged him a full adult fare, so the shortlist got cheaper per head as it got shorter.
  • 💵 $50 apart, and the gap is the group size. $376 against $426. Sidewalk is a standard walk with five stops; Secrets caps at nine guests, covers gratuities and taxes, pours a beer, and serves full portions rather than tastes.
  • 📉 Read the review counts, not just the stars. 4.7 on 26 reviews is a thinner promise than 4.9 on 304. Sidewalk answers that by naming its venues — its six carry about 3,500 Yelp reviews between them, which is the number to weigh.
  • 🏷️ Groupon: one unknown, one dead end. New Orleans Secrets has no Groupon listing at all. Sidewalk’s $89 was compared against a $79 direct price that turns out to be the child fare — the adult fare is $99 — so that one is worth re-pricing before you book.
  • 🗺️ One list is a plan, not a promise. Sidewalk visits five of the six it names and says the line-up can change; Secrets will not say where it goes at all. Neither guarantees a given stop on a given day.
