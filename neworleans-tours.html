💡 Why Standalone Swamp Tours Are Hard to Find on Gray Line

Major ticket outlets default to pushing Swamp + Plantation Combo Packages because adding a plantation stop increases ticket pricing by $40–$60.

The Solution: Top specialized local operators offer direct round-trip hotel pickup shuttles from downtown New Orleans for standalone swamp tours without forcing a plantation stop. Below are 20 curated options categorized by neighborhood, boat type, and package format.

✓ Verified 2026-08-22 — how to read the prices on this page

A price and a coupon expire, and the expiry is not printed on them. Every deal in this section carries the date it was checked and the page it was checked against, so a reader can tell a fresh number from a stale one. Cards elsewhere on this page were written in one pass in August 2026 and carry only a blanket footer claim — treat those as undated.

These dates are day-granular, and that is a deliberate limit rather than an omission. A stamp reading 2026-08-22 means someone opened the seller's page that day. It does not mean the price held all day — an operator can change a fare or pull a promotion between breakfast and dinner, and a day-granular stamp cannot express that and does not try to. So: a same-day stamp is worth trusting for which price was published; confirm the number itself at checkout, which is the only place it is ever authoritative. If you are reading this on a later date than the stamps, treat every figure as a starting point for a comparison, not as a quote.

Where an operator publishes a price directly, that is the number shown, because operator-direct is usually cheaper than the aggregator — and where it is not, that is said too. No promo code appears here unless it was found on the seller's own page. Discount mechanisms (free-tour-plus-tip, advance purchase, combo, city pass, kids-free ages) are preferred over codes: they rot far more slowly.

✓ Verified = checked on the named page, on the named date ◆ Mechanism = a durable discount, not an expiring code ✗ Unverified = claim could not be confirmed at source struck = a retracted figure, kept on purpose — never a live price

If you are auditing this page with a script: corrections keep the old number visible, because a correction without its killed claim makes the next reader re-derive the killed claim. That means a preserved wrong price sits inside a live card. Every historical figure is wrapped in <s class="retracted"> — exclude that selector before extracting prices, or the first dollar figure you match on a corrected card will be the retracted one. Two independent audits have already been fooled by this, and both were the auditor's regex rather than the page.

💡 6. Crucial Booking Tips for a Group of 4
