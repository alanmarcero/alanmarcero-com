💡 6. Crucial Booking Tips for a Group of 4
  • 🚫 NEVER take Uber/Lyft to the Swamp Rideshare to rural bayous (Slidell/Lafitte/Marrero) works going out, but drivers will NOT accept return rides back to New Orleans. Always select the official operator shuttle add-on.
  • 📞 Call Operators for Direct Price Matching If you see a Groupon or Viator deal, call the operator directly. Most will match the discount over the phone, giving you better cancellation flexibility and saving them commission fees.
  • ⏰ Confirm Hotel Pickup 24–48 Hours Ahead When booking a shuttle package, call or email 24–48 hours prior to lock in your exact 15-minute pickup window outside your hotel lobby.
  • 🐊 Morning vs Afternoon Swamp Tours In September, morning tours (8:30 AM – 9:30 AM) offer cooler weather and far active alligators before the afternoon heat sets in.
